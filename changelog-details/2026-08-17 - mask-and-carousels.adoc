= Mask mark and tool carousels

RepoLab’s mark is now a Venetian carnival mask: the product is a face over other forges and engines.

* Nav, footer, and favicon use the mask silhouette (Delapouite / game-icons.net, CC BY 3.0).
* Homepage hero cuts the mask out of a drifting field of host and VCS marks.
* Two snap carousels list git hosts and version-control engines, with icons on the providers page too.
